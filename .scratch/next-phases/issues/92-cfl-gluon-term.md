# Should the CFL phase carry a free thermal gluon gas at all?

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: -
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

Raised by [ticket 82](82-alphabag-gluons-default.md) and left out of its
ruling, which was about a DEFAULT rather than about which sectors the paired
phase has.

`eos/alphabag/solver.py::solve_cfl` adds the full free-gas gluon term whenever
`include_gluons` is set:

    if include_gluons:
        gluon = gluon_thermo(T, alpha)
        P_total += gluon.P; e_total += gluon.e; s_total += gluon.s

`gluon_thermo` is 16 massless bosons at mu = 0 with the factor
1 - 15 alpha_s/(4 pi) — the UNPAIRED phase's gluon gas.

**The model's own document says that is not the paired phase's physics.**
`eos/alphabag/alphabag.md` (and `.tex` at the same place):

> The gluon term is not part of (Pcfl). In the CFL phase the gluons are all
> massive through the Meissner effect and their thermal population is
> suppressed; the sector remains available as a flag at the solver level and
> is added to the totals there, as it is in the unpaired phase, but it is not
> inside the phase's own potential.

So the behaviour is *declared*, not silent — but "available as a flag" is a
weak defence for a term the same paragraph calls suppressed. And the two
locked-phase models disagree about it: `eos/abpr/species.py` REFUSES `gluons`
and gives this exact physics as the reason —

> "a thermal sector, identically zero at T = 0, and in the CFL phase the
> gluons are Meissner-massive besides"

— while `alphabag`'s `cfl` mode adds the unsuppressed gas on request.

**What ticket 82 already changed.** `gluons` now defaults `False`, so no `cfl`
call inherits the term; a caller who gets it has named it. That closes the
silent half and is why this is not urgent.

**Candidate answers.**

1. **Suppress it properly** — give the CFL branch a Meissner-massive gluon
   thermodynamics (a mass gap in the Bose integral) rather than the massless
   gas. Correct physics, most work, and needs a source for the masses.
2. **Refuse it in `cfl`** — `solve_cfl` raises on `include_gluons=True`, the
   way `abpr` refuses the flag outright. Cheapest honest answer, and it makes
   the two locked-phase models agree.
3. **Keep it and say so louder** — the document already declares it; leave the
   physics and treat the flag as the caller's explicit responsibility.

**Cost is measurable and small.** The two places the notebooks build a CFL
table (`notebooks/quark_eos.py:745` and `:1136`) are both at T = 0, where the
gluon gas is identically zero, so no shipped figure depends on the answer.
`test/baseline/case_alphabag`'s `cfl.*` rows are also T = 0. At T = 30 the term
is P = 0.1187 MeV/fm^3 against a CFL pressure of 173.0.

Recommend 2 unless a Meissner-mass source is to hand, on the ground that a
sector the document calls suppressed should not be reachable in the closed
form that assumes it is not.


## Ruling

Agreed with the user after a grilling: **candidate 2 — the `cfl` mode refuses
the gluon sector — and the ruling reaches a second flag the ticket had not
named.** `thermal_neutrinos` is refused there too.

### Three of this ticket's own premises were wrong, and two of them mattered

**1. `abpr` is not the independent second opinion the ticket took it for.**
`abpr/species.py:32-40` refuses `photons`, `gluons` AND `thermal_neutrinos`
with the same PRIMARY reason — "identically zero at T = 0, which is the only
temperature this model has". Meissner is a "**besides**". So abpr's ruling is
about temperature, not about the paired phase's sectors, and the ticket's
"two locked-phase models, opposite answers" overstated it. The proof that
abpr's T = 0 reasons do not bind alphaBag is already in the repository:
alphaBag's `cfl` mode carries PHOTONS at T > 0 and nobody has called that a
contradiction. Only the "besides" clause reaches this question — which is
fine, because that clause is the physics, and the physics is what was ruled
on. **abpr's gluon message also ended "for T > 0 use the 'cfl' mode of
eos.alphabag"**, a pointer this ruling falsifies; corrected here.

**2. The physics distinguishes gluons from photons, and "both are thermal
sectors" cannot.** Colour-flavour locking breaks SU(3)_c x U(1)_Q to a single
unbroken U(1)_Qtilde, generated by a mixture of the electric charge and the
eighth colour generator. Of the NINE gauge bosons exactly ONE stays massless —
the rotated photon, to which the medium is transparent — and all eight gluons
acquire Meissner masses. So `photons=True` in `cfl` is right at leading order
(a free massless gas of two polarizations, thermodynamically the same
`photon_thermo(T)`), while `gluons=True` adds `g_g = 2 x 8 = 16` massless
degrees of freedom that are the wrong COUNT as well as the wrong dispersion.
That asymmetry is the argument, and candidate 3 ("keep it and say so louder")
cannot state it: the document would have to say the sector is available in a
phase where its degrees of freedom do not exist.

**3. Candidate 1 is not "add a Meissner mass".** The light bosonic sector of a
CFL phase is the superfluid PHONON (1 degree of freedom, v = 1/sqrt(3) at
leading order) plus the pseudo-Goldstone meson octet — a DIFFERENT sector, not
a corrected one. Massive gluons are not what should replace the term. That
puts candidate 1 well past this map's destination; it is recorded in
`docs/DEFERRED.md` as the sector it actually is.

### What candidate 2 costs: measured, and it is nothing

Through `eos_point`, `mode="cfl"`, `Parameters.default()`, at Delta0 = 50 and
100 and n_B = 0.45 / 0.80 / 1.30 — the term is a free gas at mu = 0, so it is
independent of both n_B and Delta0 and every row moves by the same amount:

| T | P_gluon | Delta P | Delta P / P | Delta s / s_matter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | **0, exactly** | 0 | 0 |
| 10 | 5.86335e-04 | +1.465838e-03 | 5e-06 – 4e-05 | 0.2 – 0.3 % |
| 30 | 0.1187329 | +1.187329e-01 | 2.9e-04 – 5.5e-03 | 1.4 – 3.0 % |
| 60 | 1.8997267 | +1.899727e+00 | 4.5e-03 – 5.1e-02 | 5.2 – 10.5 % |

**"It is small" was never the argument for keeping it.** At T = 60 and
n_B = 0.45 the term is 5 % of the pressure and 10 % of the entropy of a phase
whose entropy it has no business being in.

**And it is measured nowhere**, which is why the change is free:

- `test/baseline/case_alphabag` builds its six `cfl.*` rows at **T = 0.0**
  (`generate_baseline.py:556-557`), where the gas is identically zero.
  **0 keys move** — confirmed, not assumed: `test_baseline[alphabag]` is green
  in the change arm of the isolated pair.
- Both notebook CFL builds are at **T = 0** (`quark_eos.py:745`, `:1136`), and
  `KNOBS.species` (`quark_eos.py:133`) does not list `gluons` at all, so they
  already passed `False`. **0 figures move.**
- The three `verify/` CFL sites: `run_full_check.py:130` and `:340` already
  pass `include_gluons=False`; `:390` takes the default but reads only
  `.error` and `.converged`, which no additive thermal sector can touch.
- `test/alphabag/test_alphabag_cfl.py:37,63` already pass `include_gluons=False`.

### The second flag, folded in rather than filed

`table.solve_at`'s `cfl` arm passed `include_photons` and `include_gluons`
down and **silently dropped `species.thermal_neutrinos`**. So
`eos_point(par, "cfl", SpeciesFlags(thermal_neutrinos=True), ...)` returned a
result that had ignored the request — §4's "a NotImplementedError is never
turned into a silent no-op", live, at the same five lines this ticket was
about. `docs/DEFERRED.md` recorded the PHYSICS gap (the paired phase has never
carried the gas; closing it moves published CFL tables) but not the silence.

The physics gap is unchanged and still deferred. Only the reporting moved: the
`cfl` arm now raises naming the DEFERRED entry. Answering one flag while
leaving its neighbour silently swallowed at the same five lines is the shape
that produced this ticket in the first place.

### The mechanism, and why the flag does NOT change category

`include_gluons` keeps its place on `solve_cfl` and **defaults `False` and
raises on `True`**, rather than being deleted. Same size as deleting it, zero
call-site churn (every existing caller passes `False` or nothing), and a
direct caller of the public `solve_cfl` gets a message instead of a
`TypeError`. Ticket 90 deletes it into the flags either way; it now finds one
fewer pass-through to translate and the reason already written down.

**Ticket 82's two-category rule survives untouched, and this is the one thing
worth reading twice.** A refusal in one mode looks like the third category 82
abolished. It is not: **82's rule is about the FLAG, this refusal is about the
PHASE.** `alphabag.gluons` still has two legal values judged over the modes the
model has — a default in the three unpaired modes, pinned by
`test_the_gluon_flag_is_still_a_default_in_the_unpaired_modes` — and `cfl`
refuses the sector because a locked phase does not contain it. That is the
same statement `abpr` makes by refusing the flag outright; `abpr` IS that
phase and nothing else, so there the phase's statement and the flag's category
coincide. §3 already holds this ground — `cfl` "is not a choice of equilibrium
condition but a statement about which phase the model describes".

The drift check `test_every_species_flag_defaults_off_or_raises` is
**untouched and needs no exemption**: it iterates flag DEFAULTS, and a
mode-conditional raise neither weakens it nor appears in it. That is exactly
the property ticket 82 chose its rule for, and it is why this ruling does not
reopen it.

### The one thing that DID break, and it is a real cost

`test_the_legacy_settings_interface_still_drives_both_phases` failed. The
legacy `TableSettings` shim carries **one** set of sector switches for both
phases, defaulting `include_gluons=True` and `include_thermal_neutrinos=True`,
so `compute_table(TableSettings(phase='cfl'))` now raises where it used to
return a table. Its docstring says what that path is: "the 2fam PNS study
writes its quark tables through this".

Fixed by naming both flags `False` at the one call site, with a comment saying
why, rather than by making the shim drop them — dropping them silently is the
defect this ticket closed. **The honest consequence, recorded in
`docs/DEFERRED.md` rather than glossed: the published first-generation CFL
tables DO contain the free gluon gas, so the shim can no longer reproduce them
at T > 0.** The shim's defaults are left alone because they are the unpaired
path's legacy defaults and are unaffected.
